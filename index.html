Formula used:
Basic = higher of (Basic % × Remuneration) or (State Minimum Wage)
Remuneration = Basic + Earnings + Excluded Allowance + Reimbursements + Employer PF
CTC = Remuneration + Employer ESI + Gratuity + Benefits
Cash in Hand (CIH) = Remuneration − Employer PF − Employee PF − Employee ESI

Statutory Bonus = Bonus % × higher of (Bonus Calculation Floor, State Minimum Wage) — per the Payment of Bonus Act, not % of actual Basic.
Employer/Employee ESI here follows your reference workbook's rule of being based on Basic (not Gross). Employee PF/ESI are this tool's own addition, needed to compute Cash in Hand — mirrored off the Employer PF/ESI rules; adjust if your policy differs.

Salary Components — Waterfall

Computed in order after Basic, Employer PF and Bonus are set aside from Remuneration. Each component is tagged with its Wage-Code category (Earnings / Excluded Allowance / Reimbursement) — that tag drives which section it appears in on the Salary Calculator. Use ↑↓ to reorder — order affects how much balance is left for later components. Residual Allowance should normally stay last.

Benefits — Fixed Additions to CTC

Company-provided benefits (insurance, meal cards, accommodation, transport, etc.) added directly to CTC — these are not part of Remuneration and are not paid out as cash.
